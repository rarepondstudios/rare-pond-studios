Color Looks

A live, read-only preview of every color look, rendered exactly as the two sites render it from the shared data/colorlooks.json. Each look is shown the way the site actually applies it for its kind: a Film look shows the full themed film-page treatment (accent, background washes, kicker, tagline and title) that the film page applies; Basics and Special looks show the bubble / banner glow they drive (Pinwheel for bubbles, Stream for wide headers); a Rentals category shows its single flat colour. So what you see here is what a visitor sees. Color looks are now edited in NocoDB (the color_looks table), changes publish to both sites automatically. This page is view-only. Hit Reload to pull the latest published values.

Color Picker

A handy utility for choosing a hex. Pick a color, fine-tune the hex, and click Copy hex - then paste it into a colour field (c1/c2/c3) on the look's row in NocoDB → color_looks. The row of tints/shades gives you lighter and darker variants; click any one to copy it.
