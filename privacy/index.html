Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 6, 2026

This policy explains what information Rare Pond Studios collects when you use rarepond.com — including the Studio, Rentals, and Media pages — how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

Rare Pond Studios is an independent film studio operated as a sole proprietorship by Jack Carlsen, based in the Los Angeles / Burbank, California area. In this policy, "we," "us," and "our" mean Rare Pond Studios. This policy covers the website at rarepond.com and its sub-sections at /rentals and /media.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

When you use our contact form, rental-request form, or crew-inquiry form, you may provide:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number;
  • Your company or production name, and details about your project;
  • For a rental request: the equipment you select and the pickup and return dates you request.

Information collected automatically

  • Your IP address, browser and device type, and the pages you view;
  • Cookie and similar identifiers set by the services described in Section 4.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and prepare a rental quote or project proposal;
  • To schedule equipment pickups and returns;
  • To keep a record of our conversation and any booking in our customer-management tools;
  • To operate, secure, and improve the website.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not send marketing or newsletter emails. When you contact us, a real person replies to you directly about your request.

4. Cookies and tracking technologies

Our contact and inquiry forms are provided by HubSpot and Jotform, which set cookies when their forms load. HubSpot's cookies (for example __hstc, hubspotutk, __hssc, and __hssrc) help those forms function and let HubSpot recognize a returning visitor; HubSpot may collect information about your activity over time and across other websites that use HubSpot. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may set cookies necessary for security and performance.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect how the embedded forms work.

5. Third parties who process your information

We share information only with the service providers that help us run the site and respond to you. We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

HubSpotContact form, customer-relationship management, and form analytics. May set tracking cookies and collect activity across sites over time.
JotformHosts our rental-request and crew-inquiry forms and delivers your submission to us.
Google CalendarStores rental pickup and return events created from your request so we can schedule your booking.
CloudflareWebsite hosting, content delivery, and security.
SupabaseDatabase that stores rental booking records.

Each of these providers processes your information under its own privacy policy.

6. "Do Not Track" signals

We do not currently respond to browser "Do Not Track" (DNT) signals, because there is no common industry standard for how to interpret them. Third-party services embedded on our site (such as HubSpot) may collect information as described above regardless of a DNT setting.

7. Reviewing, changing, or deleting your information

You may ask us to review, correct, or delete the information you submitted, or the record we hold about your inquiry. Email contact@rarepond.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Data retention and security

We keep your information for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry, fulfill a booking, and meet ordinary business and record-keeping needs, then delete or archive it. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information we hold, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Children

This site is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. California residents

Because of our size, Rare Pond Studios is not a "business" required to comply with the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA). We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA. California residents may still contact us with any privacy question or request, and we will do our best to honor it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change, we will post the updated policy at this URL with a new effective date. Please check back from time to time.
